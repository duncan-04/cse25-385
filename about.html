About Us

Establishing solutions that impact lives

We aim at improving the livelihoods of individuals in society by reducing air pollution in our environment.

At PowerUp Batteries, we implement and power electric vehicles to reduce fossil emissions that contribute to climate change.

Mission: Provide high-quality, affordable, and sustainable battery solutions for electric vehicles.

Vision: To be a leading provider of innovative battery technologies that empower clean mobility worldwide.

The company was founded in 2025 to manufacture electric vehicle batteries for various EV companies. We are committed to sustainability and environmental responsibility.

Our team consists of experienced professionals in battery technology, engineering, and sustainability.

We are proud of our achievements and continue to innovate and grow.

Area of Focus

We develop high-performance batteries to enhance EV range, efficiency, and sustainability.

We are also exploring artificial intelligence in battery management systems to optimize performance and extend battery life.

Sustainability

We minimize environmental impact through responsible sourcing, efficient manufacturing, and recycling initiatives.

We actively support green technology incentives and innovation.
