Types of Electronic Vehicle Batteries

Lithium-ion

PowerUp Batteries supplies advanced lithium-ion batteries to global automotive manufacturers. We are the #1 li-ion battery supplier in the global market for hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and full-electric vehicles. Our advanced technology is designed to provide improved energy density, lower costs, and improved driving range — all intended to reduce the vehicle's environmental impact.

Nickel Metal Hydride

PowerUp Batteries began developing Ni-MH battery cells for HEVs in 2020, and started mass production in 2025. Our battery cells are designed to be durable and to maintain a high charging and discharging capacity over an extended period of time. We employ unique battery manufacturing technologies, including polar plate sintering technology.
