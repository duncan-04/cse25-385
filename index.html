Lithium-ion

PowerUp Batteries supplies advanced lithium-ion batteries to a number of global automotive manufacturers and is #1 li-ion battery supplier in the global market for hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and full-electric vehicles. PowerUp's advanced Lithium-ion battery technology is designed to provide improved energy density, lower costs, and improved driving range, all intended to reduce the vehicle's environmental impact.

Nickel Metal Hydride

PowerUp Batteries began developing Ni-MH battery cells for HEVs in 2020, and started mass production in 2025. Our battery cells are designed to be durable and to maintain a high charging and discharging capacity over an extended period of time. Additionally, we employ unique battery manufacturing technologies, including polar plate sintering technology.
