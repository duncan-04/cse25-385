    Electronic Vehicle Battery Packs

    PowerUp Batteries, with its diverse product lineup and optimal solutions, delivers long driving range, high reliability, and quick charging in the EV market.

    Battery Electric Vehicle

    A fully electric car.
    Power Source: Uses battery only
    Charging: Use a plug-in charger
    100% electricity, No fuel

    Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicle / Extended Range Electric Vehicle

    A mix of electric + fuel engine.
    Power Source: Battery + petrol engine
    Charging: Plug-in charger + engine can recharge battery.
    It drives on electricity first then switches to fuel when battery is low.

    Hybrid Electric Vehicle

    A hybrid but not a plug-in.
    Power Source: Battery + fuel engine
    Charging: Battery charges automatically (regenerative braking).
    Uses both engine and battery together.

    Mild Hybrid Electric Vehicle

    A small electric assist system.
    Power Source: Fuel engine + small battery
    Charging: Automatic
    Battery helps engine and cannot drive on electricity alone.
